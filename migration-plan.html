Key Legal Advantage

EU Free Movement (Directive 2004/38/EC)

Sister & daughter hold Dutch (EU) passports. When an EU citizen moves to a different EU member state, their non-EU spouse gets automatic residence rights.

What this means: Husband needs NO language exam, NO income proof, and NO visa sponsorship in any EU country except the Netherlands (which applies its own national law). This opens 26 EU countries.

Netherlands workaround: Move to Belgium first (3–6 months), then relocate to NL invoking EU law — bypasses the Dutch language exam entirely. Alternatively, A1 Dutch is basic and achievable in 3–4 months.
