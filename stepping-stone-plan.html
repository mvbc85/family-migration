The Plan: Phase by Phase

Month 0–1 · Launch

Arrive in Tallinn. Register as EU citizen (sister) + apply for husband's family member card. Find apartment. Enrol daughter in school (Estonian public or international). Open bank account (digital, takes 10 minutes).

Month 1–4 · Stabilize

Sister job hunts in Tallinn (cost control, finance, shared service centre roles). Husband continues his remote admin work. Daughter settles into school. Living off the loan buffer.

Month 4–18 · Build

Sister working. Loan repayment begins (~A$1,000/month). Building European CV, professional references, LinkedIn network. Learning how EU workplaces operate. Maybe picking up some Dutch on the side.

Month 12–18 · Hunt

Start applying for NL/DE/IE jobs. Now her CV says "Senior Cost Controller, [Company], Tallinn" — not "Accenture Argentina". She can fly to Amsterdam for interviews (€60 return, 2.5hr flight). Recruiters take her seriously.

Month 18–24 · Upgrade

Accepts offer in Netherlands/Germany. Moves (cheap intra-EU). Employer may offer relocation package. Dutch salary = loan repayment accelerates to ~A$2,000/month.

Month 24–30 · Done

Loan fully repaid. Family settled in target country with European experience, established careers, and daughter bilingual in English.

Key concern: Winter darkness (sunset at 3:30pm in December) can affect mental health. Seasonal depression is real and common among immigrants from sunny countries.

Mitigations: Vitamin D supplements, light therapy lamps (~€30), well-heated apartments, warm clothing. Estonians are masters of making winter cozy indoors. The daughter will barely notice — kids adapt fast.

Reframe: If this is just 18–24 months before moving to Netherlands, they only experience 1–2 Estonian winters. Manageable.

If We Decide Yes — Next Steps

Step 1 · Agree terms

Put it in writing (even informal): amount, interest (6% or 0%), monthly repayment start date, what happens if they're late.

Step 2 · She starts job hunting

She can begin applying to Tallinn roles from Argentina NOW (LinkedIn, CV.ee, MeetFrank). May even secure something before arriving.

Step 3 · Book flights

Buenos Aires → Tallinn. Often cheapest via Istanbul (Turkish Airlines) or Helsinki (Finnair). ~$1,200–1,500/person.

Step 4 · Transfer funds

Send in tranches if more comfortable: $15k before departure, $10k after arrival + registration confirmed. Use Wise for best rates.

Step 5 · They arrive

Register at police station (EU citizen registration, same day). Find apartment (Facebook groups, KV.ee, City24.ee). Enrol daughter.

First payment ~month 5 (after first full salary received). Monthly transfer to our account via Wise.
